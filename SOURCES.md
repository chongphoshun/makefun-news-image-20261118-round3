## Corrections made in this revision (19 Nov 2026)

- **Dow:** −566 was a preliminary figure. Reuters final: **−561.25 to
  50,198.17**. Image and "By the numbers" now say −561 everywhere.
- **Anthropic:** "$65bn listing" was the May private round. The IPO priced
  $83/share for **~$81.3bn proceeds** (largest IPO ever), ~$1.5T
  fully-diluted valuation. Image now says "an $81bn listing".
- **Gaza camps:** "Four nations demand aid access" was unsupported.
  ReliefWeb: a joint stakeout led by UK, Qatar and Jordan, **150,000
  tents awaiting entry**, and an infant died of hypothermia. Image now
  says "Cold kills; 150,000 tents blocked."
- **El Hierro:** "2,331 migrants in a weekend" was the two-day total for
  the whole Canary Islands, not El Hierro. Verified: **1,235 landed on
  El Hierro in one day** (record), 1,488 across the Canaries in 24h.
- **France:** "fourth weekend" was wrong — the 14 Nov march was the
  **fifth** consecutive weekend of cost-of-living protests.
- **Como:** the 9–1 win over Lens was a **friendly**, not a Champions
  League match. The image body never claimed otherwise.
- **Nvidia board:** source upgraded from paisakawach.com to AP/Barchart.
